So I don't expect any miracles to come out of the A.I. IPOs. I only want 
to see the worst of the wealthy that made terrible investments in this 
field down and out. Doesn't matter who. Rich Arabs, Jews. Rich Germans, French. 
Rich Chinese, Japanese, Vietnamese. Rich Brazilians, Mexicans, Colombians. 
If their investments were really that bad, and I mean that for the progress 
of the field as well as the morality, as subjective as that sounds, then 
they should lose money that they can't recover. And then they should exit 
from investing in the field anymore. 

Truthfully, there should be a big mainstream media announcement: 
> A.I. Winter Has Hit. Extremely Cold Front From Northern Eurasia To Set
> In For At Least A Decade.

I'm not excited for the A.I. IPOs to make me millions...millions of dollars 
that I can put into a large estate and live somewhere off for good. I hope 
it's not just me that thinks the antics of a lot of people during this past 
year and a half concerning A.I. have been mighty annoying. Like "in your face" 
annoying annoying. It's just too much, and it's mainly metaphysics from my 
end. The media that's been put out for A.I. is intent on drawing people in 
for all these perspectives and insights to be gathered. Besides from the 
heavy intellectualism of the field, which is not that frequently found in 
social media, there's been: 
- hystericism from the many corners of socioeconomic ideology,
- insanely positivist and optimistic accounting of the hot A.I. news, 
- one person pointing out how foolish another person that's talking about
  A.I. is,
- insane capital numbers,
- this East-West rivalry, which I don't think is really as serious as it is
  annoying in that kind of cross-cultural way, kinda like how a person asks
  another "how do you say f* you" in language X.
- this kind of p**sing contest.

I've been thinking through this from my end. GPUs. Missing. That about sums up 
the "real" A.I. potential available to people such as me. And the fanaticism of 
some people that think there are so many breakthroughs going on now. That to me 
is more appalling than the advancements themselves. You know how some people, 
they think they're so spiritual or something after being hopped up on all the 
rave drugs, the ones that claim they have one breakthrough after another breakthrough 
after another, getting wiser and smarter and wiser every time, every time racking up 
threes and tens of sexual partners in the process. That's how I interpret the 
big "WHOOP WHOOP AI is advancing so fast!" claims. The gall of some people 
demands they assume their audience is a terminally naive teenage audience. It's 
just not cool. The field is not supposed to be this big get-together, filled with 
universal friendship and great great sincerity shared between people. And that's 
because humanity is actually flawed, stranger or not. Like seriously. 

The regulation of the A.I. industry should get up to par after the IPOs. Also, 
maybe a short time after that, people can actually rediscover themselves through 
different careers or something. I personally have lost a good part of my trust with 
this A.I. thing. It's just not the best thing for the changing times. I don't want my 
career to be entirely for the development of A.I. I am looking to depart from the 
eyes and ears of some people, mainly because of the annoyances, fickleness, and 
uselessness.
